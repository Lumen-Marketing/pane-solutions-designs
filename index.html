About the Instagram reels

All three pages embed the three real reels from @pane_solutions_llc using Instagram's official embed widget. Instagram's player does not auto-play — it shows the cover frame with a play button and the viewer taps to watch. Until the widget loads, each panel shows the real cover frame with a link straight to the reel, so nothing ever renders as an empty box.

If you want the clips genuinely auto-playing and looping with no Instagram branding, send the three MP4 files (save them from the Instagram app — your own reel → ⋯ → Save) and they can be swapped in.

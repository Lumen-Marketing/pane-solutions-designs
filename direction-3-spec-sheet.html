SHEET 01 — SERVICE SCHEDULE

Scope of
work

Three line items. Expand any one for what's actually included.

Glass cleaned inside and out, with the tracks, sills and screens done as part of the same visit. Second-storey and hard-to-reach panes are handled with a purified water-fed pole, so there's nothing left behind to dry into spots.

  • Interior + exterior glass
  • Tracks, sills and frames wiped
  • Screens cleaned and refitted
  • Water-fed pole for high glazing
  • Walkthrough before we leave

Driveways, walkways, patios, pool decks and block walls. Desert dust, hard-water staining and sun-baked grime come off — with the pressure matched to the surface so nothing gets etched or stripped on the way.

  • Driveways and walkways
  • Patios and pool decks
  • Block walls and stucco
  • Pressure matched to the surface

Debris cleared out by hand, downspouts flushed through, and the whole run checked for sag and separation while we're already up there. Best done ahead of monsoon season rather than in the middle of it.

  • Debris cleared from the run
  • Downspouts flushed clear
  • Run checked for sag and separation
  • Ground left clean
